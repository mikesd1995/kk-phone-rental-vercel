1. Enquiries and booking confirmation

Select an event and package, then review the prepared LINE message before sending. Please provide accurate details needed for the rental, such as the event, date, phone or package, pickup time, nickname and contact information.

Availability and prices displayed on the website are for enquiry purposes. Wait for the shop to confirm the particular booking, price, pickup and return arrangements through LINE. A sold-out event or waitlist enquiry does not guarantee that equipment will become available.

On your first visit in this browser, you must read and explicitly check the current rental Terms before browsing events. The website remembers only the version identifier locally to avoid repeated prompts; it does not append a legal acknowledgement to your LINE booking draft. Browser confirmation is not a LINE customer-specific acceptance receipt. The shop may ask for additional confirmation before finalising a rental where necessary.

2. Prices and payment

Confirm the phone model, included equipment, rental price and promotion conditions with the shop before accepting a booking. If you request additional accessories, ask the shop to confirm the total price and included items.

According to the current website FAQ, payment is collected when you pick up the equipment at the event. During the current promotion, the shop does not charge a deposit or a separate device insurance fee if you follow the shop on X. Confirm the promotion applicable to your particular booking.

Opening LINE from this website does not process a payment. This page has no card-payment form.

3. Equipment pickup, return and documents

Pickup and return take place at the event venue. Confirm the meeting point and times with the shop through LINE.

According to the FAQ, the standard rental equipment includes a phone, phone bag and power bank. A lens add-on includes a lens bag and cleaning cloth. Check the actual items with the shop at pickup and return.

The FAQ describes a photograph of a national ID card, driving licence or passport alongside event-entry evidence, and a photograph with the phone and accessories at pickup. Before sending personal documents, ask which details are necessary and what may be covered or redacted. See the Privacy Policy for information about how personal data is used.

4. Changes and cancellations

To change an event, date, time or package, or to cancel, contact the shop on LINE with the original booking details and wait for confirmation of the requested change.

If any cancellation, refund or case-specific charges apply, the shop must communicate and agree them with you before that booking is confirmed. This page does not introduce new cancellation fees or refund deadlines. If an event is postponed or cancelled, contact the shop about the options applicable to your specific agreement and circumstances.

5. Equipment and problems during rental

Inspect the condition and completeness of the equipment together with the shop at pickup and return. If equipment malfunctions, is damaged or is lost during use, contact the shop through LINE so the situation can be reviewed and addressed.

Any specific responsibility or charges relating to equipment must be communicated and agreed before the rental, taking account of the circumstances and applicable law. These Terms do not remove rights you have under applicable law.

6. Photo and video delivery

If your rental includes delivery via Google Drive, download the delivered files promptly. According to the FAQ, access is provided for approximately 30 days, after which the link may no longer be available.

Google Drive previews may display reduced-quality images; download the actual files to view the delivered quality. This delivery-access window is separate from retention periods for booking records, conversations and evidence under the Privacy Policy.

Browsing packages or submitting a rental enquiry through LINE does not require a Google account. Personal data you give the shop for booking, pickup and return is used for purposes explained in our Privacy Policy, which also covers sharing, correction and deletion requests.

When you follow links to LINE, X, Google Drive or other websites, the relevant external service's terms may also apply. Visiting this website or accepting these service Terms is not blanket consent to process personal data for every purpose.
