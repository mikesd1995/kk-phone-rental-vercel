PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy

Effective 6 November 2026

KK Phone Rental uses information you provide to handle enquiries, bookings, pickup and return of equipment, and delivery of files under agreed services. This page explains how we handle that information.

You can browse this website and read these documents without signing in or connecting a Google account. The booking button prepares a message for LINE for you to review and send. The shop confirms bookings through the conversation.

1. Shop and contact details

This service operates under the name KK Phone Rental. For questions about personal data, or to ask us to review, correct or delete information, contact the shop's LINE Official Account and identify your message as a “personal data request”.

Contact LINE OA · @827blnmo

2. Information associated with the service

  • Contact and bookings: Your name or nickname, contact channel, phone number if provided, event, date, venue, package, pickup time, booking status, allocated equipment and coordination details.
  • Payment: Payment status, amounts received and amounts related to your rental. The public website does not collect payment card details through a form.
  • Rental evidence: According to the shop FAQ, this may include an image of an ID card, driving licence or passport together with event-entry evidence, and a photograph with the equipment at pickup. Ask which details are necessary and which may be obscured before sending; do not send more information or documents than needed.
  • Messages and images sent to the shop: Chat text, display name, images, timestamps and conversation references supplied by chat providers. They may include a separate rental Terms statement if you send one yourself. The website does not automatically append this statement to a booking draft. The first-visit browser confirmation is stored locally and is not linked to your LINE ID or identity.
  • Technical usage information: Website access requests and error records necessary for service delivery, as well as browser data described in section 6.

Some booking and identity-verification information is necessary for checking availability, contacting you and handing over equipment. If you do not provide necessary information, the shop may not be able to process your enquiry or provide the rental. Please contact the shop before sending anything that concerns you.

3. Purposes of processing

The shop uses relevant information to receive and answer enquiries, check availability, process pre-contract requests and agreed rentals, identify the person collecting equipment, coordinate pickup and return, record payment, and resolve problems relating to a rental.

Operational records also support security, accuracy checks, troubleshooting and compliance with legal obligations. Each use must have an appropriate legal basis. Where consent is required, we must provide the relevant information and obtain it separately. Reading this website or accepting rental Terms is not consent to every kind of personal data processing.

4. Google services

Customers do not need to sign in to or connect a Google account to browse packages or enquire through LINE. The shop uses Google services to manage rental information and deliver relevant files.

For a Google account connection authorised by the shop's administrator, the system may be able to access, read and edit Google Sheets available to that authorised account, manage Apps Script operations, and make requests to connected services. The technical permissions are not necessarily restricted to a single spreadsheet; actual access and onward use should be limited to authorised operational needs.

Data obtained from Google APIs is used only for disclosed, authorised functions in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not sell such information, use it for targeted advertising or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Any sharing with relevant service providers is limited to what is needed and any applicable permitted exceptions, such as security or legal requirements.

An account holder who authorised access can review or revoke connected-app access through Google Account connections. Revoking access does not automatically delete information the shop has already retained. To request deletion, contact us as explained in section 8.

5. Service providers and information sharing

The shop uses external providers as needed, for example LINE for communication and booking confirmation, Google for information management and file delivery, and website or storage providers for booking operations and security. The data shared depends on the service used; not every provider receives every category of information.

Relevant providers may process limited information as needed to deliver the service and for lawful operations. Some may process information outside Thailand. When you follow a link to an external website or app, that provider's own privacy policy also applies to your use of its service.

6. Website and browser data

The website may store language, light or dark appearance settings and temporary information to support your experience. For LINE booking links, the website uses localStorage and a functional first-party cookie to remember only the version of rental Terms explicitly confirmed in this browser. The cookie lasts up to 180 days, holds no name, phone number, LINE ID or booking details, and is not a legal acceptance receipt or a completed booking. A new Terms version requires a fresh confirmation. You can clear browser storage in your settings; browsers that block storage may ask again. Clearing browser data does not erase booking enquiries or messages already sent to the shop.

Website providers may process access and error information necessary to operate the service. If the shop adds further analytics tools, relevant information and any legally required consent arrangements must be updated before those tools are used.

7. Retention and deletion

Photos and videos delivered through Google Drive: According to the FAQ, customers can access delivered files for approximately 30 days. Download files promptly. This period concerns access to delivered files; it is not an automatic deletion deadline for every type of personal information.

Bookings, customer and payment information, chats, evidence and records: Retention may be necessary to administer rentals, review disputes and fulfil applicable legal obligations. Moving an old item to a history or archive is not equivalent to deleting it. Different records and backups may have different necessary retention periods and constraints. For questions about a specific record or a deletion request, contact the shop.

Deletion from the shop's systems may be separate from messages or files kept in your account or by an external provider. Revoking Google access or clearing your browser is not a substitute for requesting deletion of information already held by the shop.

8. Your rights and how to submit a request

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access and obtain a copy of your information, request corrections, data portability, object to processing, restrict processing, request deletion or de-identification, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect lawful processing already performed and may affect services that require the relevant information.

Contact the LINE Official Account with enough detail for us to locate the relevant records, such as the event or booking date. Do not send additional identity documents before the shop explains why they are needed. We may need to verify your authority to make the request and explain if the request cannot be fully fulfilled due to an applicable legal duty or the need to establish or defend a claim.

Submit a personal data request · LINE @827blnmo

You may lodge a complaint with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee Office as permitted by applicable law if you believe your personal information is being processed unlawfully.

9. Policy changes

The shop will display the date of any updated policy here. If the purposes or handling of information change in a way that requires additional notice or consent, we must provide that notice or obtain the required consent before the new use.
