# KK Phone Rental — Product Contract

## Mission
Help concert/event customers rent suitable phone equipment with low friction and trustworthy event-specific information. The product must remain useful as inventory expands beyond any single phone model.

## Primary customer job
Find the event -> understand the available rental option and verified price -> know pickup/return and key policies -> contact/book with confidence.

## Product principles
- Event-first, not device-first.
- Mobile-first and fast under real mobile conditions.
- Verified truth beats persuasive copy.
- Thai and English are first-class.
- Human approval remains required where existing governed flows require it.
- Small-inventory reality matters: do not design as if supply is unlimited.
- Automation should reduce repetitive work without creating a second source of truth.

## Current value proposition
Customer-facing benefits may include no-deposit/free-insurance, venue pickup/return with guidance, delivery/storage of captured files, and eligible customer promotions only when backed by current business truth.

## Core product surfaces
1. Storefront discovery and event cards.
2. Search/filter and event detail/decision information.
3. Booking/contact flow.
4. Admin operational surfaces.
5. Scanner/Verifier governed event pipeline.
6. Pricing/availability/inventory truth.
7. Owner review/approval notifications and evidence.

## Non-goals
- Becoming a generic ecommerce catalogue.
- Coupling the brand to one device model.
- Autonomous mutation of pricing, bookings, inventory or customer communication outside governed authority.
- Adding AI because it is fashionable when deterministic logic is safer/cheaper.

## Success
Success means customers can make the rental decision quickly and correctly, while operators can discover events, maintain truth and recover from failures without hidden/manual state divergence.
